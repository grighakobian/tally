Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

The short version: Tally does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Everything you calculate stays on your device.

This policy covers the Tally calculator apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Android, including their Home Screen widgets (together, “Tally”).

Data we collect

None. Tally has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Tally does not connect to the internet; the Android app does not even request internet access.

Data stored on your device

To work, Tally keeps a small amount of information on your device only. It is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Your settings, such as whether key sounds are on.
  • Widget state: the number currently shown on each Home Screen widget, so it is still there the next time you look.
  • Calculation history and memory, kept in memory while the app is running.

Deleting Tally removes all of this.

Clipboard

Tally reads or writes your clipboard only when you choose Copy or Paste on the display. When you paste, Tally only uses the text if it is a number.

Sounds and haptics

Key clicks and haptic feedback are played locally on your device. Tally does not use the microphone.

Third parties

Tally contains no third-party SDKs, analytics or advertising libraries. Your App Store or Google Play account is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

Children

Tally does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
