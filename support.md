# Support

Help with Tally on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Android.

> **Need help or found a bug?** [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/grighakobian/tally/issues/new) and include your device, its system version and what you were doing. You can also browse [existing questions](https://github.com/grighakobian/tally/issues).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get the scientific keys?

On iPhone, turn your phone sideways. On iPad and Mac, they appear when the window is wide enough.

### How do I add a widget?

- **iPhone and iPad:** touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, tap _Edit_ › _Add Widget_, search for _Tally_ and choose a size.
- **Android:** touch and hold the home screen, tap _Widgets_ and drag a Tally widget into place.

### Why does the widget react a little slower than the app?

Widgets are redrawn by the system after each tap, so pause briefly between key presses.

### How do I turn off the key click sounds?

Long-press the display and choose _Sound Off_. On iPhone you can also use Settings › Tally › Key Sounds; on Mac, Tally › Settings. Clicks are always silent when your device is in Silent mode.

### How do I copy or paste a number?

Long-press the display and choose _Copy_ or _Paste_. On Mac, use ⌘C and ⌘V or right-click the display.

### How do I clear everything at once?

Tap _AC_, or touch and hold the delete key (⌫).

### Can I use my keyboard on Mac?

Yes. Type digits and `+ − * / ^ ( ) %`, press Return or `=` for the result, Delete to erase and Esc or C to clear.

### Does Tally collect my data?

No. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).
