Support

Help with Tally on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Android.

Need help or found a bug? Open an issue on GitHub and include your device, its system version and what you were doing. You can also browse existing questions.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get the scientific keys?

On iPhone, turn your phone sideways. On iPad and Mac, they appear when the window is wide enough.

How do I add a widget?

iPhone and iPad: touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, search for Tally and choose a size.
Android: touch and hold the home screen, tap Widgets and drag a Tally widget into place.

Why does the widget react a little slower than the app?

Widgets are redrawn by the system after each tap, so pause briefly between key presses.

How do I turn off the key click sounds?

Long-press the display and choose Sound Off. On iPhone you can also use Settings › Tally › Key Sounds; on Mac, Tally › Settings. Clicks are always silent when your device is in Silent mode.

How do I copy or paste a number?

Long-press the display and choose Copy or Paste. On Mac, use ⌘C and ⌘V or right-click the display.

How do I clear everything at once?

Tap AC, or touch and hold the delete key (⌫).

Can I use my keyboard on Mac?

Yes. Type digits and + − * / ^ ( ) %, press Return or = for the result, Delete to erase and Esc or C to clear.

Does Tally collect my data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.
